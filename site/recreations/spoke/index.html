Reach summits, not repair shops

    

      
Proactive wear tracking
Be the first to know when a chain, pad or tyre is near its limit, from ride data and your service log.

      
Faster time to saddle
Ships from the workshop closest to you, with depots across the north, the south, Scotland and Wales.

      
Build confidence with fit
Get a professional fit with every frame, and a fit file you can take to any shop.

      
Managed spare parts
Get the right spares sent automatically, scaled to how far you ride, with no waiting period.

      
Flat-free guarantee
Tubeless set-up on every wheel, sealant checks with each service, and a roadside callout if it fails.

      
Route and weather alerts
Know about closures, flooding and wind on your usual routes before you clip in.

      
Verify every part
Each component is logged against your frame number, so a stolen bike is easy to prove and trace.

      
Ride-tested frames
Every frame is fatigue-tested well past standard, and ridden by our own team before it ships.

      
Theft-proof by design
Hidden tracking in the frame and a lock that talks to your phone, insured from day one.
